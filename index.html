About Millie Bobby Brown

Millie Bobby Brown is a British actress and producer. She became internationally famous for playing Eleven in the Netflix series Stranger Things. She has also starred in several films and founded Florence by Mills.

Acting

She is known for her roles in fantasy, mystery, adventure, and science fiction projects.

Producing

She has worked as both an actress and a producer on the Enola Holmes films.

Florence by Mills

Her brand offers beauty and personal care products.

This is an unofficial fan website and is not affiliated with Millie Bobby Brown or her representatives.

Stranger Things — Eleven

Eleven, also known as Jane Hopper, is a central character in the mysteries surrounding Hawkins. She has psychokinetic abilities, and her story explores friendship, courage, identity, and belonging.

Personality

Brave, determined, protective, and constantly discovering more about herself.

Important Relationships

Her connections with Mike, Will, Max, Dustin, Lucas, Hopper, and Joyce are important to the story.

The World of Hawkins

Secret experiments, the Upside Down, and the adventures of the friend group are key parts of the series.

Enola Holmes

Millie plays Enola, the younger sister of Sherlock Holmes. Enola is clever, curious, and independent. She follows clues, solves mysteries, and challenges society's expectations as she finds her own path.

Enola Holmes (2020)

Enola searches for her missing mother and discovers her own talents along the way.

Enola Holmes 2 (2022)

Enola opens her own detective agency and takes on a new case.
